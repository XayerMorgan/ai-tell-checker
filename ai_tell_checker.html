Help & About

What this tool does

This tool scans text for patterns documented in frequency studies, tokenizer analyses, and editorial research as disproportionately present in AI-generated writing. It pairs raw stylistic density with a weighted multi-signal AI Certainty model to differentiate between genuine AI boilerplate and isolated human rhetorical habits.

It does not determine authorship. Light human editing easily alters heuristic signals. This is an editorial triage assistant designed to highlight passages worth a second look.

How AI Certainty & Granular Measures are Calculated

A naive flat count (e.g. 15 flags = "High") is flawed because an 18th-century speech with 15 parallel triads is completely different from a text saturated with ChatGPT stock transitions.

Our model evaluates three dimensions:

  • 1. Granular Diagnostic Weights ($w_i$):
    • Tier 1 • 4.0x High Diagnostic: Stock AI boilerplate transitions and Corporate AI filler. Almost never appear naturally in formal vintage or academic human writing.
    • Tier 2 • 2.0x Moderate Diagnostic: Post-ChatGPT vocabulary spikes (delve, tapestry, multifaceted) and Reflexive Hedging softeners.
    • Tier 3 • 0.5x – 1.0x Secondary / Contextual: Rule-of-three comma triads, Em dash / double-hyphen density, and Markdown emphasis. These occur organically in classical oratory and human essay writing.
  • 2. Multi-Signal Corroboration ($C$): Real AI output systematically trips multiple distinct categories at once. If a text trips only weak secondary structural patterns with zero Tier 1 markers, a heavy dampener ($C = 0.35 imes$) is applied. When 4+ independent categories fire, the multiplier increases ($1.25 imes ext{–} 1.50 imes$).
  • 3. Composite Probability Mapping: The composite index ($I = D_w imes C$) is mapped via a calibrated sigmoidal curve into an estimated probability percentage (Low <25%, Uncertain 25–59%, High ≥60%).

Why would 14.2 per 1,000 be Low Probability? (Declaration of Independence Case Study)

The National Archives Stone Engraving transcription of the Declaration of Independence (1,335 words) trips 19 raw flags (14.2 raw flags/1k words):

  • 15 Rule-of-three / rhetorical triads (Tier 3 • 0.5x): Classical 18th-century oratorical pacing ("plundered our seas, ravaged our Coasts, burnt our towns", "works of death, desolation and tyranny", "levy War, conclude Peace, contract Alliances").
  • 3 ASCII double-hyphens (Tier 3 • 0.5x): Stone engraving pause notation (--That to secure...).
  • 1 Lexical word (Tier 2 • 2.0x): The word "Boundaries".
  • 0 Stock AI phrases (Tier 1) & 0 Corporate filler phrases (Tier 1).

The Verdict: Under the weighted composite model, the Declaration scores ~14% (LOW PROBABILITY). The system recognizes that despite high rhetorical density, the complete absence of Tier 1 AI boilerplate and the lack of multi-category corroboration mean this is an isolated stylistic pattern, not AI generation.

Categories explained

Tier 1 • 4.0x Stock AI phrases
Boilerplate transitional phrases common in generated essay- and report-style prose: "in today's fast-paced world," "it's worth noting that," "a testament to," "in the realm of."
e.g. "In today's fast-paced world, it's worth noting that..."
Tier 1 • 4.0x Corporate / email AI patterns
Filler and softening phrases specific to AI-generated professional email/memo tone: "circle back," "as we navigate," "gentle reminder," "please don't hesitate to reach out."
e.g. "I hope this finds you well. As we navigate the coming quarter..."
Tier 2 • 2.0x Lexical overuse (banned vocabulary)
Words with statistically significant post-ChatGPT frequency spikes in published research (e.g., "delve," "underscore," "meticulous," "commendable," "tapestry").
e.g. "We delved into the intricate landscape of..."
Tier 2 • 2.0x Reflexive hedging
Softening counterpoints attached to nearly every claim ("however," "that said," "it's important to note"), indicating model uncertainty avoidance.
Tier 3 • 1.0x Contrast-framing rhetoric
"It was not X, it was Y" / "not just X, X" constructions, a repetitive dramatic sentence structure favored in AI marketing/narrative copy.
Tier 3 • 0.5x Rule-of-three lists
Three-item comma sequences used as a rhythmic device ("furious, frightened, undone"). Excludes Title-Case proper noun sequences as of v1.2.0.
Tier 3 • 0.5x Em dash / double hyphen density
Frequent single-token punctuation in GPT-4 family models. Flagged as a secondary signal only due to heavy natural use by human writers.
Tier 3 • 0.5x Markdown emphasis markers
Excessive bold/italic formatting on standard statements.
